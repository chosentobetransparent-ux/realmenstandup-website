About the Ministry

The Movement

Will The Real Men Please Stand Up is the men's ministry of Chosen To Be Transparent Church — a brotherhood built to raise responsible, spiritually grounded, emotionally healthy, purpose-driven men who strengthen their families, their church, and their community.

Male by birth. Man by choice. Manhood by development.

Led by
Apostle Calvin Miller, Chosen To Be Transparent Church
Mission
Reinforce families. Restore accountability. Awaken what God placed inside every man.
Vision
Men walking in identity, integrity, and legacy through teaching, brotherhood, and accountability.

This is not a club. It's a commissioning.

We are not here to tell men everything that's wrong with them.

We are here to awaken what God placed inside them.

Real men don't just show up — they stand up.

About the Founder

The Man Behind the Mandate

Apostle Calvin R. Miller Sr.

Founder · Senior Leader, Chosen To Be Transparent Church

Apostle Calvin Miller is a husband, father, U.S. Navy veteran, author, and entrepreneur. He leads Chosen To Be Transparent Church and 1Team1Fight Ministries in Houston, Texas, and carries a mandate to see men stop hiding, stop shrinking, and stand up into the mantle of manhood God assigned them.

He is the author of The Real Man's Path: Male By Birth, Man By Choice — The Mantle of Manhood and its companion 30-day journal, and he teaches men every Tuesday morning at 5AM — because real men report for duty before the world wakes up.

He is married to Pastor Helen Miller, founder of She SOARS Women's Ministry, and together they model faith, resilience, and purpose in life and legacy.
