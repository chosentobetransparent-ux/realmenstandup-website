Our Vision

To see every man come down from this retreat standing male by birth, man by choice — fully awake to his God-given identity as priest, prophet, protector, pastor, and provider, carrying the mantle of biblical manhood back into his home, his church, and his city.

The Grounds

Lakefront lodge on Lake Conroe — swim spa, bonfires, BBQ, yard games, fishing off the dock, and room for the whole brotherhood. Come prepared to relax, be imparted into, and fed spiritually, mentally, and physically.
