The Five Habits of Faith · Nehemiah 6 · Series Complete

Nehemiah had every invitation to come down off the wall — distraction, slander, fear, and false counsel. He finished anyway. Five habits that keep a man on his assignment when the enemy sends for him.

  • I Focus — the great work outranks the invitation (Neh 6:1–4)
  • II Discernment — reading Gashmu before you answer him (Neh 6:5–8)
  • III Prayer — “Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands” (Neh 6:9)
  • IV Courage — “Should such a man as I flee?” (Neh 6:10–14)
  • V Finishing — “So the wall was finished” (Neh 6:15–16)
