Today's charge

Stay On Your Wall

“And I sent messengers unto them, saying, I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down: why should the work cease, whilst I leave it, and come down to you?”Nehemiah 6:3

Sanballat sent for Nehemiah four times, and four times the answer was the same: I cannot come down. Brother, something has been sending for you too — an old habit, an old argument, an old version of you. It doesn't deserve a meeting. Your family, your assignment, and your God are on the wall with you.

This week's action: name the one thing that keeps calling you down off your wall — and tell a brother about it before Sunday. What gets named gets guarded.
